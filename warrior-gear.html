30–42 · Scarlet Monastery and Razorfen Downs

One Undercity stop. Pick up Into The Scarlet Monastery from Varimathras in the Royal Quarter and Bring the End from Andrew Brownell in the Magic Quarter. Both are available at 37.

55–60 · Gear for your first level-60 dungeons

Do atRewardWhere to get it
55–57Guidance of UrsocAt 47 and Neutral with Timbermaw Hold, take The Twin Bears from Lorekeeper Nok in Ursolan, Azshara. Get both totems in Blackmaw Hold before the one-way drop.
56–58Omokk’s Girth Restrainer
or Voone’s Vice GripsChoose one		At 55, speak to Warlord Goretooth in Kargath, Badlands. Use his letter to start Warlord’s Command. Kill Omokk, Voone and Wyrmthalak and loot the documents in Lower Blackrock Spire. The gloves give 1.5% hit.
58BloodseekerAt 58, take The Battle for Alterac from Voggah Deathgrip outside the Horde Alterac Valley tunnel. Defeat Vanndar and choose the crossbow from Hero of the Frostwolf.
58–60Mark of FordringAt 52, take Demon Dogs, Blood Tinged Skies and Carrion Grubbage from Tirion Fordring in north-west Eastern Plaguelands. Later, recover the painting in Stratholme Living and do the In Dreams escort in Hearthglen.
58–60Band of the PenitentAt 55, take Houses of the Holy from Leonid Barthalomew at Light’s Hope Chapel. Open supply crates for five Stratholme Holy Water on your painting run.
58–60Blackhand’s BreadthStart with Warlord’s Command at 55. After Lower Blackrock Spire, speak to Eitrigg and Thrall in Orgrimmar. Kill Rend in Upper Blackrock Spire for For The Horde!.
58–60Blinkstrike ArmguardsAt 57, take The Darkstone Tablet from Shadowmage Vivian Lagrave in Kargath. Collect the tablet in the Upper Blackrock Spire egg room on your Rend run.
58–60Mirah’s SongAt 55, speak to Eva Sarkhoff outside Scholomance in Caer Darrow. Do Krastinov, Jandice and Kirtonos in that order, returning to Eva between steps.
58–60Crystal-Infused CleaverAt 55, take The Dark Crystal from Master Apothecary Faranell, Undercity. Loot the Drained Crystal Fragment beside the Achromatic Crystal in The Shaper’s Terrace, Un’Goro Crater.
60Gordok’s HandguardsAt 56, take Unfinished Gordok Business from Captain Kromcrush after a Dire Maul North Tribute run. Loot the gauntlet in Dire Maul West after Prince Tortheldrin. Turn it in while still King of the Gordok.

The quest gear route from 45 onwards is based on Cynara’s Warrior quest rewards video.
