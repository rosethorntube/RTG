Rosethorn's WoW Levelling Optimiser — dungeon quests
Catalogue checked 25 November 2026. WoW Classic Forever, both factions.

The website groups quest facts by levelling run, pickup stage and faction.
Required quests, alternative prerequisites, quest-in-log requirements and
optional introductions are represented separately. Suggested character levels
are Rosethorn's route recommendations. Individual quest records link to the
Forever database by quest ID. Each dungeon also links to its source catalogue.

Guide-specific changes include separating overlapping instance footprints into
runnable wings, distinguishing pickups from post-run follow-ups, and excluding
obsolete entries, raid-only duplicates and area-tagged city errands. Dungeon
and quest names belong to Blizzard Entertainment. No live client is connected
to the website. Completion marks are saved in the player's browser. See the
associated sharing evidence for the sources of the party-sharing flags.

Party sharing
The catalogue retains the source record supporting each sharing label. A group
member must still qualify for the quest, including any prerequisite turn-ins.
The sharing mask is 0x8. Where a source omitted the field, a separate
Forever record with an explicit value was required.

Additional yes/no checks use individual Forever quest records, including
Wowhead, Zephras, Efficient Gaming, SenseOS, 60.tools
and ForeverChanges. These are source-recorded flags and are not a log of
personal in-game tests. Five Ruins records have conflicting published
values (95189, 95195, 95204, 97289 and 97292). For each, Wowhead's exact-ID
Quick Facts and two independently published build-tagged records agree that
it cannot be shared. Players should collect these personally.
